Atomic Drift Tuner — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 3, 2026

1. Overview

Atomic Drift Tuner (“Atomic,” “Atomic Bot,” “we,” “our,” or “the Service”) provides Discord integrations and tools related to the Atomic Drift Tuner project.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Atomic Bot may access, how that information is used, and how it is handled when you interact with the bot.

By using Atomic Bot, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Atomic Bot May Access

Atomic Bot is designed to collect and retain as little personal information as reasonably necessary.

When you interact with Atomic Bot through Discord, Discord may provide information necessary to process the interaction, which may include:

* Your Discord user ID
* Your Discord username and display name
* The Discord server ID where a command was used
* The Discord channel associated with an interaction
* Slash-command selections and information you intentionally submit to the bot
* Basic Discord interaction metadata

Atomic Bot does not require access to the contents of ordinary Discord conversations for its standard slash-command functionality.

3. Information You Submit

Some future Atomic Bot features may allow users to voluntarily submit information, including:

* Bug reports
* Feature requests
* Assetto Corsa car information
* Car-pack information
* Hardware information
* Atomic Drift Tuner version information
* Diagnostic information
* Testing or compatibility reports

Information submitted through these features may be stored or transferred to services used to operate and develop Atomic Drift Tuner, including GitHub.

For example, information submitted through a bug-report command may be used to create or update a GitHub Issue.

Users should not submit passwords, authentication tokens, API keys, private account information, or other sensitive personal information through Atomic Bot.

4. How Information Is Used

Information accessed or submitted through Atomic Bot may be used to:

* Respond to Discord slash commands and interactions
* Provide Atomic Drift Tuner release and download information
* Provide supported-car and car-pack information
* Diagnose problems with Atomic Drift Tuner
* Process bug reports
* Process feature requests
* Improve car and car-pack detection
* Coordinate beta testing
* Maintain and improve Atomic Drift Tuner and Atomic Bot
* Prevent abuse or misuse of the Service

Atomic Bot does not sell personal information.

5. GitHub Integration

Atomic Drift Tuner uses GitHub for software development, releases, issue tracking, and related project management.

Certain information voluntarily submitted through Atomic Bot may be transferred to GitHub when necessary to provide a requested feature.

For example, submitting a public bug report or feature request may result in information from that submission appearing in the Atomic Drift Tuner GitHub repository.

Users will be informed when a feature is intended to create publicly visible GitHub content whenever reasonably practical.

6. Data Retention

Atomic Bot retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide its functionality, maintain the project, resolve problems, prevent abuse, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Some information intentionally submitted to public project resources, such as GitHub Issues, may remain available as part of the project’s development history.

7. Data Sharing

Atomic does not sell or rent personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services when necessary to operate Atomic Bot and Atomic Drift Tuner. These services may include:

* Discord
* GitHub
* Infrastructure or hosting providers used to operate Atomic Bot

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, protect the Service, investigate abuse, or protect the rights and safety of users or others.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect credentials and information associated with Atomic Bot.

Bot tokens, webhook credentials, API keys, and other authentication secrets are not intentionally stored in the public Atomic Drift Tuner source repository.

No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children’s Privacy

Atomic Bot is intended to be used in accordance with Discord’s applicable age requirements and policies.

Atomic does not knowingly use the Service to intentionally collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

10. Third-Party Services

Atomic Bot operates through Discord and may interact with GitHub and other third-party services.

Use of those services is also subject to their respective terms and privacy policies. Atomic Drift Tuner does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Atomic Drift Tuner and Atomic Bot evolve.

Material changes may be announced through the Atomic Drift Tuner project, Discord server, GitHub repository, or other appropriate channels.

The “Last Updated” date at the top of this document will indicate when this policy was most recently revised.

12. Contact

Questions, concerns, or requests relating to this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the official Atomic Drift Tuner GitHub repository or the official Atomic Drift Tuner Discord community.
